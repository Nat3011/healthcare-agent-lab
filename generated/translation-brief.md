# Patient information translation

User: Patient communications team

Problem: Approved patient information may be inaccessible in the reader’s language.

Workflow: Draft a translation of approved administrative information for bilingual review.

Input: Fictional appointment preparation and access instructions.

Output: A translation draft and a list of terms requiring verification.

Measure: Meaning preserved and critical terms checked by a qualified reviewer.

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: HHS AI Use Case Inventory FY25, CSV records 186 (header counted as record 1)
https://www.hhs.gov/programs/topic-sites/ai/use-cases/index.html

Adaptation: HHS reports translation support in Smartling. This is an adaptation to patient communication, not evidence of medical translation accuracy.

Score: 90/100. Initial analyst judgement, not validated ROI.
