# Referral and document intake

User: Referral coordinator

Problem: Staff manually create cases from incoming documents and requests.

Workflow: Extract administrative fields, propose a case record and flag missing information.

Input: Fictional referral forms and an explicit required-field checklist.

Output: A proposed intake record and exceptions for staff approval.

Measure: Correct field extraction and missed required fields.

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.
