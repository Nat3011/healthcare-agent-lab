# Clinical guidance search

User: Clinical operations staff

Problem: Staff lose time locating current approved guidance across documents.

Workflow: Search a small approved guidance library and return cited passages.

Input: Fictional clinic procedures and approved administrative FAQs.

Output: A cited answer with source version and an unable-to-answer state.

Measure: Citation accuracy and time to find the approved passage.

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: HHS AI Use Case Inventory FY25, CSV records 27, 51, 154 (header counted as record 1)
https://www.hhs.gov/programs/topic-sites/ai/use-cases/index.html

Adaptation: HHS entries concern search and IIS or programme guidance. Adapted to approved clinic material; no independent clinical advice.

Score: 93/100. Initial analyst judgement, not validated ROI.
