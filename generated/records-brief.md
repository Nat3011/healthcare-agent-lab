# Medical record summary

User: Medical records reviewer

Problem: Reviewing long medical records takes time and makes supporting evidence hard to locate.

Workflow: Create a dated summary with links to the supplied record passages.

Input: Three fictional visit notes and a referral letter.

Output: A chronology, missing-information list and passage references.

Measure: Summary completeness and incorrect statements per record.

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: HHS AI Use Case Inventory FY25, CSV records 290 (header counted as record 1)
https://www.hhs.gov/programs/topic-sites/ai/use-cases/index.html

Adaptation: The HHS entry concerns claims review. This prototype adapts document condensation to clinician-reviewed records; it gives no diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

Score: 97/100. Initial analyst judgement, not validated ROI.
